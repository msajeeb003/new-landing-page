# Keyword & AI-recommendation plan: mostafizur.me

Research done 6 Nov 2026: Google autocomplete (US and Bangladesh), live Google results for every target keyword,
competitor pages, "best of" lists, directories and communities. Google Trends was unavailable during research, so
demand is judged from autocomplete (a suggestion only appears when enough people search it) and from what ranks.

---

## 1. What the research found

1. **"AI builder" and "best AI builder" are tool searches, not people searches.** Autocomplete for "AI builder" is
   Microsoft AI Builder, Salesforce, Power Automate, "AI builder website/app" and the AI Builder Cup.
   "Best AI builder" means "best AI app or website builder tool" (Base44, Replit, Wix…). Nobody typing these wants
   to hire a person, so ranking for them would bring no clients. **We don't chase them.**
2. **"Best AI builder in Bangladesh", "best freelance AI developer", "top AI developers in Bangladesh" have almost
   no Google searches** (no autocomplete at all). Google shows company directories for them (Clutch, GoodFirms,
   DesignRush, Tracxn). But these are exactly the questions people ask **ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Copilot**,
   and those answer from third-party pages: directories, LinkedIn, Upwork, Reddit, Facebook groups, articles.
   **These are AI-recommendation targets, won mostly off-site.**
3. **Searches with real demand that fit you:** "AI automation expert in Bangladesh" (an autocomplete suggestion),
   "n8n expert in Bangladesh" (suggestion), "AI engineer Bangladesh", "hire freelance AI developer", "freelance AI
   agent developer", "AI agent builder freelancer", "AI automation freelancer", "hire AI automation expert",
   "n8n expert freelancer", "Zapier expert for hire", "hire Make.com expert", "AI MVP development", "AI SaaS developer".
4. **Individuals can rank for the Bangladesh searches.** For "AI automation expert in Bangladesh", a one-person site
   (tanvirahmed.com.bd, title "AI Automation Engineer in Bangladesh") is #3 and a LinkedIn profile is #4. For
   "n8n expert in Bangladesh", a personal blog post (shafiqur.dev) ranks. Their advantages are simple: the exact
   phrase in the title, case studies, a blog, and many linked profiles. Nothing you can't match or beat.
5. **"Hire freelance AI developer", "n8n expert for hire" are owned by marketplaces** (Upwork, Toptal, Arc, Fiverr,
   Contra, Freelancer). You win these by ranking **inside** those platforms, not with your own site.
6. **Your name is shared with a stronger AI expert.** Google's #1 for "Mostafizur Rahman AI builder" is
   Dr. Mostafizur Rahman, Chief AI Technologist (UK), with LinkedIn, Google Scholar and ResearchGate. Your GitHub
   is #3; mostafizur.me is not in the top 10. The plain name is professors and the cricketer Mustafizur Rahman.
   **AI assistants can confuse you with him. Fixing this is job #1 for AI recommendations.**
7. **Google has indexed 6 of your pages** (home, about, projects, SignPro, SociPRO, AI automation). The homepage
   still shows the old title "Gen AI Engineer & Automation Specialist"; it updates when Google recrawls.

     and "vibe coding" wording where it's true.
2. **New page: `/hire-ai-developer-bangladesh`** (`Hire an AI Developer in Bangladesh | Mostafizur Rahman`). It answers
   a client's real questions: what I build, US/UK/EU time-zone overlap, English, how a project runs, case studies,

   clients or reviews.
3. **Clear identity for Google and AI assistants:**

     Kushtia; builds AI apps and n8n/Zapier/Make automations; GitHub msajeeb003"), `hasOccupation` (AI Builder,
     located in Bangladesh) and `areaServed` on services (USA, UK, EU).
   - A "Who is Mostafizur Rahman?" FAQ answer and an identity block in `llms.txt` with the same unique facts.
   - No nickname. The name stays "Mostafizur Rahman"; what tells you apart is the same descriptor everywhere:
     **"Mostafizur Rahman, AI builder in Kushtia, Bangladesh"**, plus the GitHub handle `msajeeb003` and mostafizur.me.

## 5. What we won't do

- Claim to be "the best" on the site. "Best" lists come from other people; self-made claims don't help and can hurt.
- Write a self-ranked "top AI builders in Bangladesh" list, buy links, post fake reviews, or stuff keywords.
- Create near-identical pages for every city or tool (Google treats those as doorway pages).
- Invent rates, clients, numbers or testimonials.
